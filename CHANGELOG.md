# Changelog

All notable changes to TaskSession CRM. Full history: **[tasksession.com/changelog](https://www.tasksession.com/changelog/)**

> TaskSession CRM ships as one codebase with Free and Pro editions. Some items below are Pro features — see the [Free vs Pro table](README.md#free-vs-pro).

## [4.10] — September 2026
- AI Assistant add-on: assistant chat, AI agents for scheduled checks, daily brief, AI Assist for descriptions
- Deep Search across modules
- REST tokens for external API access

## [4.9] — August 2026
- Browser push notifications for chat, tasks and projects (incl. iPhone/iPad, Safari iOS 16.4+)
- Per-device subscription management

## [4.8] — August 2026
- Activity page with search and filter tabs
- Notifications with read / view all actions
- Staff dashboard with tasks, documents and leads

## [4.7] — August 2026
- Voice notes in chat
- Client single and bulk invoice payments with CSRF protection

## [4.6] — August 2026
- Mega search in the header and dedicated search page
- Stripe subscription auto-charge and saved client cards
- Menu settings for header visibility

## [4.5] — May 2026
- WooCommerce ecommerce module (stores, orders, customers, products)

## [4.4] — July 2026
- Company profile pages, company grid and linked companies
- Attendance improvements (holidays, overtime, manual entries)

## [4.3] — June 2026
- Email Marketing add-on with drag-and-drop email builder, audiences and tracking

## [4.2] — May 2026
- Server Health Monitor (CPU, RAM, disk, slow requests)

## [4.1] — May 2026
- Redesigned project overview, archive tasks on Kanban, upcoming deadline filter

## [4.0] — June 2026
- Invoices for client companies, recurring invoices, default memo and footer

For versions 1.0 – 3.31, see the [full changelog](https://www.tasksession.com/changelog/).
